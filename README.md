## The pitch

> You know that app idea you've been carrying around for months — just describe it in plain words on make.fun and an AI agent will build your first working version for free, no code, no cost, nothing to lose.

## Why this pitch

The pitch was written to match the house judge's criteria, in order:

1. **One read, one reason.** It speaks straight to the friend's own idea ("that app idea you've been carrying around for months") and gives the reason to say yes in the same breath.
2. **Persuasion.** The ask is tiny — just describe the idea in plain words — and the risk is spelled out as zero three ways: "no code, no cost, nothing to lose."
3. **Clarity.** One sentence, everyday words, no jargon. A non-coder reads it once and knows exactly what to do.
4. **Truth.** It promises only a *first working version*, built for free — what an AI agent on make.fun can actually do. No deadline is promised, no magic claimed.
5. **Warmth and craft.** It opens like a friend talking ("You know that app idea..."), not a slogan, and the closing triplet gives it a rhythm of its own.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (creating `/output` if needed). The program is deterministic, uses no network, and reads no environment variables or other files.
